PUBLISHED · 2023

In-doped AlSb: optoelectronic response.

A computational investigation of composition-dependent structural, electronic and optical properties.

RESEARCH QUESTION

How does In alloying change electronic and optical properties?

This paper studies In-doped aluminium antimonide as a potential optoelectronic material. The verified journal article is linked here; individual computational figures will be added only from approved originals or properly permitted reproductions.

Methodology

The journal article reports first-principles FP-LAPW calculations of Al₁₋ₓInₓSb and describes the treatment of structural, electronic and optical properties.

Portfolio materials

This page can host original or permitted band-structure plots, density of states, optical-response graphs, simulation inputs, and analysis notes. Figures are intentionally not reproduced from the journal PDF without permission.
