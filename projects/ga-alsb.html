PUBLISHED · 2022

Ga-doped AlSb: electronic & optical properties.

A first-principles study of compositional changes in a III–V semiconductor alloy.

RESEARCH QUESTION

How does Ga alloying affect AlSb properties?

This study investigates structural quantities, electronic bands, density of states, and optical response in Al₁₋ₓGaₓSb. The paper reports composition-dependent changes in its band gap and optical behavior.

Methodology

The published paper employs full-potential linearized augmented plane wave (FP-LAPW) calculations; GGA-PBE and a TB-mBJ approach are described for structural and electronic/optical analysis.
