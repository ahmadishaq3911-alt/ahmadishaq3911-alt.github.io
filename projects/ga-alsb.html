PUBLISHED RESEARCH / 2022

Gallium-alloyed AlSb.
A different response.

How composition changes structure, electronic states, and optical behavior in a III–V semiconductor alloy.

THE MATERIALS QUESTION

What changes when
gallium enters the lattice?

This study uses first-principles FP-LAPW calculations to investigate Al₁₋ₓGaₓSb.

Structural properties are treated with the generalized gradient approximation. Electronic and optical behavior is studied using GGA and the TB-mBJ approach.

The paper reports a composition-driven change from an indirect to a direct band gap, together with changes in optical response.
