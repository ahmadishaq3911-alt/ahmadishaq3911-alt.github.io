My BS (Honors) in Physics specialized in Digital Electronics. I later taught Digital Electronics in BS and M.Sc. programs.

The supplied laboratory material covers half and full adders, half and full subtractors, and RS, JK, D, and T flip-flops. Implementations use XOR, AND, OR, NOT, NAND, and NOR gates, including IC 7486, 7432, 7408, and 7400.

The workflow connects a circuit diagram to gate checks, input combinations, observed outputs, and truth-table verification.

From the laboratory material.

Combinational circuits

Half and full adders produce sum and carry bits. Subtractors produce difference and borrow bits. Alternative NAND/NOR implementations connect Boolean algebra with gate-level design.

Sequential circuits

Flip-flops introduce memory: output depends on inputs and the previous state. RS, JK, D, and T devices illustrate setting, resetting, storing, and toggling a bit.
