MY BUILD PROCESS

From a diagram to an output.

Read & assemble

Identify the inputs and outputs, check the gates, and make the connections from the circuit diagram.

Apply & observe

Apply the input combinations and record the corresponding sum, carry, difference, borrow, or stored state.

Compare & verify

Compare the observed outputs with the truth table to check whether the circuit implements the intended logic.

TRY A RELATED CIRCUIT

Three input bits.
Two output bits.

A full adder adds A, B, and a carry-in bit. The sum is the low bit; carry-out is the high bit.

Switch the inputs to explore all eight combinations. For example, A = 1, B = 1, and carry-in = 0 gives sum = 0 and carry-out = 1: binary 10.

This demonstration explains the logic. The photograph above shows my breadboard work; it does not identify a specific gate topology.
