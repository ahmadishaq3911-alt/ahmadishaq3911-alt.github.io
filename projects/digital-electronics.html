ELECTRONICS / PROJECT ARCHIVE

Digital electronics & circuit design.

An organized home for verified university-era electronics projects, design reports, circuit diagrams, and teaching demonstrations.

BACKGROUND

Hardware thinking alongside physics.

My earlier academic background and teaching experience include digital electronics, logical reasoning, circuit-oriented instruction, and semiconductor fundamentals. This collection is designed to present individual projects clearly once the original source materials are identified.

How each project will be documented

1. Problem and purpose

State what the circuit was designed to do, for which course or application.

2. Circuit schematic

Upload an original or recreated schematic and explain key components.

3. Design and testing

Explain the logic table, timing, simulation or bench test that was actually performed.

4. Results and reflection

Include verified waveforms, photographs, observations and your specific contribution.

REPOSITORY ORGANIZATION

Use the separate digital-electronics-projects GitHub repository. It contains a reusable template for adding each confirmed project.

No specific undergraduate project names or technical claims are invented here.
