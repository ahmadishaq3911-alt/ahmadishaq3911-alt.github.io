Conceptual 2D and 3D artwork

The layered-materials hero and top-down graphene illustration were created for this website using the built-in image-generation tool. These are artistic explanations, not DFT structures, measured results, calculated charge densities, or evidence of a topological state.

Interactive atomic model

The model uses a regular honeycomb lattice with a conceptual neighboring layer. Coordinates and layer separation are arbitrary. It is designed to explain geometry and perspective, not to reproduce a relaxed heterostructure.
